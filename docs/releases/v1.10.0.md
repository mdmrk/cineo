## New

- **Linux: Cineo updates itself.** As on Windows, the sidebar offers "Update to vX.Y.Z" when a new version is out; Cineo checks the signature and checksums, installs it, and "Restart to update" starts the new version.
- **The update shows its download progress.**
- **New streaming stats panel**, with trend graphs and a close button.

## Changed

- The desktop executable is now called `cineo` (`cineo.exe` on Windows). The installers remove the old `cineo-desktop`, and the in-app update works with either name.
- Updated libmpv on Windows and FFmpeg, dav1d and HarfBuzz on Android.

## Fixed

- A click on the video toggles pause even if the pointer moves slightly during the click.
- Torrents: seeking no longer waits for pieces requested for the old position when the player closes the old connection late.
