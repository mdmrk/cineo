## New

- **Remote control from your phone.** Cineo on Android controls playback in Cineo on a computer on the same local network: play and pause, seek, volume and mute, audio and subtitle tracks, subtitle delay, speed and stop. On the desktop it is on by default in Settings → Remote control; pair the phone by scanning the QR code, opening a `cineo:///remote/` link or typing the address and code.
- **Play on the computer.** Each playable stream on the phone's detail page has a computer button that starts it on the computer, and Continue Watching and Library items offer "Play on the computer" to resume there. Torrents still need the computer's own P2P consent.
- **Now playing bar on the phone.** While the computer plays something, a bar above the navigation shows its title, progress and a play/pause button; tapping it opens the remote.
- **Found through sync.** With cloud sync on, phones in the same sync space find your computers without pairing.
- **Several computers.** Each computer is listed by name, and you choose which one the phone controls in Settings → Remote control.
- **Japanese and Korean** on the desktop and on Android.
