## Changed

- The Windows executable has its icon and version, and carries libmpv compressed, keyed by its checksum.
- The Windows build ships the license text of every library in its libmpv, FFmpeg's LGPL-3.0 and mpv's exact texts.
- The window shows its title while a torrent connects.
- Playback positions round to the nearest millisecond.
- Android: the Rust library unwinds on panic, and the APK ships the glad and fast_float notices libplacebo builds in.
- Every tag now checks that F-Droid reproduces the released APK.
