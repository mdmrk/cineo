Cineo is a native media center for Stremio-protocol addons, written in Rust. Install the addons you choose, browse and search their catalogs, and play their streams in a built-in player, over HTTP or BitTorrent. Cineo has no account, no cloud, no ads and no analytics: your library and settings stay on your phone.

Cineo ships no content. On first run it installs Cinemeta, Stremio's metadata catalog, so the home screen has something to show; every other addon is your choice.

<b>Addons</b>
<ul>
<li>Install an addon from its manifest URL, reorder addons and remove them</li>
<li>Discover addons in the Official and Community lists, and configure those that need it in your browser</li>
<li>Addons that stop loading stay listed, so you can remove them</li>
</ul>

<b>Browse and search</b>
<ul>
<li>Catalog rows from every installed addon on the home screen</li>
<li>Search across every addon that supports it</li>
<li>Stream lists you can filter by quality or addon and sort by addon order, quality, seeders or size</li>
</ul>

<b>Player</b>
<ul>
<li>Built on libmpv, with hardware decoding</li>
<li>Audio and subtitle tracks from the file, plus subtitles from addons</li>
<li>Preferred audio and subtitle languages, with a second choice for each</li>
<li>Subtitle style, seek step and controls timeout in Settings</li>
<li>A stats panel with network speed, buffer, resolution, codec and decoder</li>
<li>Resumes where you stopped</li>
</ul>

<b>Torrent streaming</b>
<ul>
<li>Cineo's own engine starts playing while the torrent downloads</li>
<li>Shows peers and speed while it connects, and a notification while a torrent is open</li>
<li>Download and upload limits, upload on or off, peer count, DHT and listening port in Settings</li>
<li>Asks before the first torrent: peer-to-peer streaming shares data with other peers and shows them your IP address. Torrent streams can be turned off entirely</li>
</ul>

<b>Library</b>
<ul>
<li>Continue watching, favourites and recently played</li>
<li>Clear the history or reset all settings from Settings</li>
</ul>

<b>Limitations</b>
<ul>
<li>English only</li>
<li>Phones with a 64-bit ARM processor (arm64-v8a); no Android TV version</li>
</ul>
