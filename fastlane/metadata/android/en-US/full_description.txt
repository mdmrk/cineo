Cineo is a native media center for Stremio-protocol addons, written in Rust. Install the addons you choose, browse and search their catalogs, and play their streams in a built-in player, over HTTP or BitTorrent. No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics: your library and settings stay on your phone.

Cineo ships no content. On first run it installs Cinemeta, Stremio's metadata catalog, so the home screen has something to show; every other addon is your choice.

<ul>
<li>Browse and search the catalogs of all your addons</li>
<li>Player built on libmpv, with hardware decoding, subtitles and audio tracks</li>
<li>Torrent streaming with Cineo's own engine: playback starts while it downloads</li>
<li>Continue watching, favourites and history</li>
<li>Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Italian, German and French</li>
</ul>

Peer-to-peer streaming shares data with other peers and shows them your IP address. Cineo asks before the first torrent, and torrent streams can be turned off entirely. You are responsible for the addons you install and what you watch through them.

For phones with a 64-bit ARM processor. Free software under the MIT license; it bundles libmpv and FFmpeg under the LGPL-2.1.
